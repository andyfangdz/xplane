# Lower flare / slower touchdown investigation

User observed fast landings and very high flare in three HUD v5 recordings. v6 commanded 80 KIAS final and began roundout at 65 ft. Actual flare entry 77.8–78.8 KIAS; touchdown 68.4–73.0 KIAS. At flare entry the undeflected trajectory intersected the runway approximately 680–790 ft from threshold, only 310–420 ft before desired 1100 ft touchdown.

TorqueSim SR20 AIM supplied with the exact aircraft, TS-SR20-AIM, pages 32–33: full-flap normal landings power on or off, power-off main-wheel touchdown; 77 KIAS short-field approach. Loading about 2942.5 versus 3050 lb maximum, square-root weight scaling of 77 = 75.6 KIAS (inference, not a printed POH speed).

Cirrus Monthly Proficiency Program July 2005, Landings, https://www.kineticlearning.com/pilots_world/cmpp/07_05/07_05_p2.html: normal powered SR20 final 75 KIAS, roundout 10–20 ft, approximately 65 KIAS touchdown. Historical training anchor, not a procedure for this steeper power-off 180.

New simulator evaluation requires 63–67 KIAS touchdown and roundout entry no higher than 35 ft, alongside unchanged 1000–1200 ft precision, 200 fpm physical sink, lateral, pitch-rate and rebound limits. These target the reported behavior and supersede the old minimum-only 67 KIAS gate. The 60 KIAS airborne abort remains. Initial development uses 75 KIAS final, 30 ft roundout and greater vertical deceleration; actual low-speed/sink/motion evidence will decide. Aircraft physics and HUD remain unchanged.

AIM page 44 (Section 5-2) visually inspected: at 3050 lb, full flaps, zero bank, published stall 61 KIAS/59 KCAS forward CG; 59 KIAS/56 KCAS aft CG. KIAS explicitly cautioned inaccurate at stall. New 63–67 KIAS contact band is above these printed indications; airborne abort remains 60. Actual simulator behavior still required.
